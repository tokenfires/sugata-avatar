## Choosing the next slice

1. Identify a concrete missing authoring or source responsibility and who will use its output. Explain how it differs from the consumed approach in the relevant receipt. A new character, label or comparison alone does not establish that difference.
2. State the decision new evidence could change, exact inputs and outputs, and a bounded stop condition. Ask the independent critic whether the experiment is worth doing before preparation or investigation beyond that scope.
3. Before execution, complete the source and workload contract, independent review and root verification. Source ownership and finite numerical checks support the decision; actual rendered images qualify appearance.
4. Preserve the original failed evidence and accepted/default assets. If the fixed first attempt fails, stop that method instead of adjusting parameters, materials, framing or the observer to manufacture a pass.
