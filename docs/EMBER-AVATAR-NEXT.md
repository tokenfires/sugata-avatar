Retain FaceBuilder as a comparison and possible alternative; its head topology is not yet selected as the final animated character. 1. Inspect existing source, inputs and jobs before any new work. Specify landmark-to-mesh correspondences and relevant controls, finite runtime/memory/output bounds and stop conditions; get the independent critic's worth decision and root verification.
2. Identify and check semantic landmarks/contours in the existing portraits. Automatic detection, if used, supplies estimates for review. Raw edges alone are ambiguous.
