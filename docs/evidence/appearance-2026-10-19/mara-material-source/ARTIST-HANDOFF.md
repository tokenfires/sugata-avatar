The atlas's pale peach/beige palette and speckled scalp differ from Mara's warm-brown face and silver groom. It is not her finished skin, a registered facial master or a geometry/age/gender claim. Whole-Mara form, intended palette, UV registration, rest-versus-expression, ocular/auxiliary/53-rig and expression roles remain open.
