# Fixed artist brief

Create a supplementary whole-character design sheet of the SAME fictional adult in the original knitwear concept. The original is the authority for visible face, warm brown skin, softly parted chin-length dark brown bob, understated expression, pale muted blue crew-neck knit, charcoal generous trousers and practical brown ankle boots. Keep the relaxed adult proportions and calm styling. Do not infer or certify perceived gender, ancestry or an actual person's identity.

Three equal full-length views on one clean warm-gray landscape page: FRONT, PROFILE, BACK. Front looks squarely at camera; profile is a genuine90degree side view facing PAGE LEFT; back directly away. Same character, proportions, clothes, grounded baseline and scale in all panels. Every head and foot fully in frame with margin. Hands hang comfortably and the sweater silhouette remains understandable. Normal far hand/foot overlap in profile is natural and not an automatic defect.

Author missing hidden clothed shape choices explicitly: gently dropped shoulder seam; soft but controlled sleeve volume to wrists with fitted rib cuffs; untucked rib hem around upper hip, overlapping the trouser waistband; loose charcoal legs with believable seat and a restrained break onto boots; quiet sweater back without extra panels/logos, practical boot heels and soles. Keep original fine matte knit interpretation; this is not a claim to reproduce boiled wool or a particular brand's manufacturing. No dramatic drape, giant balloon body, newly tight pants, jacket, skirt, accessory or replacement hairstyle.

Actual image acceptance: three unmistakably different intended views, complete bodies/hands/visible feet, recognizable visible identity/palette/outfit continuity, legible shoulder/wrist/hem/seat/leg/boot relationships, no actionable extra/missing limb or corrupted clothing. The sheet can author unregistered hidden forms; it cannot establish exact orthographic registration, anatomy/measurements, cloth physics, sewing, editable/native/3D source, body/foundation fit, rig/morph/motion or runtime/AAA qualification.

Alternatives: one supplementary sheet makes whole-look hidden design choices inspectable, but may drift; current front+separate drafts avoid that risk but leave the complete side/rear wearer unspecified. First actual result alone is judged. On any complete source/image B or unknown, retain bytes/receipt and stop with no crop, edit, regen, retake, relabel or successor.
