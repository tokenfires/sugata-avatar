## Product Details

Inspired by 90 years of shoemaking tradition, the women's Whitney Weejuns® Penny Loafer brings the effortless sophistication of the original penny loafer to every step. Crafted from premium wine leather, this timeless slip-on is distinguished by signature G.H.BASS details, including the iconic Whale Tail penny keeper, traditional hand-sewn moccasin stitching, beefroll detailing, and durable Blake stitch construction. A resoleable leather outsole ensures it's a shoe that's meant to be worn, restored, and appreciated for years to come. Relaxed enough for denim and dresses, yet elegant enough for tailored separates, the Whitney strikes the perfect balance between modern versatility and heritage craftsmanship, transitioning seamlessly from workdays to weekends.
