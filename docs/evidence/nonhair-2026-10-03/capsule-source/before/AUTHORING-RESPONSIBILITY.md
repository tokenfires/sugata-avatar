# Long-sleeve everyday prototype: authoring responsibility

This is a source-directed g050 prototype contract, not a constructed garment, shipping capsule or builder admission. Root owns isolated source authoring; the independent critic owns visual/contract review. The full male/female/neutral starter, clothing variety, expression and AAA goals remain open.

The new visual objective is a relaxed long-sleeve knit with a clear shoulder transition, room through the torso and upper arms, sleeves ending at the wrists, and a straight upper-hip hem. Its drape should make a softer, less body-clinging silhouette than the actual fitted blue source thumbnail. Straight navy trousers and the current boots complete the prototype. A logo removal or palette change alone does not meet this target. It is a design proposal; no target image or runtime frame has yet qualified it.

Actual authorable controls are in the cached MakeHuman system-assets archive: `clothes/male_casualsuit02/male_casualsuit02.obj`, `.mhclo` and `.mhmat`, with diffuse/normal/AO images. Their exact bytes, explicit CC0 headers and the MPFB basemesh `data/3dobjs/base.obj` are pinned in [source-inventory.json](source-inventory.json). The exact [long-sleeve thumbnail](inputs/male_casualsuit02.png) is a source/control witness, alongside [current short tee](inputs/female_casualsuit01.png) and [other short tee](inputs/male_casualsuit04.png). Root and critic actually inspected these images. They establish source availability and sleeve/silhouette distinctions, not Sugata fit or appearance.

The unchanged OBJ/MHCLO/material control files are copied to `inputs/male_casualsuit02/`; textures remain pinned archive members, so this small copy is not a ready-to-load installed asset. A future own-authored knit source must have its own name, topology, UV/material work and source history. The control OBJ contains no named groups; explicitly separating top/trousers and establishing their ownership is part of that source work. Do not assume the old MHCLO mapping survives changed vertices or topology. Runtime fragments are outputs, not the authoring source.

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Body | Keep the exact accepted g050 body and pose controls. The helper basemesh is an authoring scaffold, not a substituted runtime body. No identity or neutral-representation inference. |
| Foundation | Preserve the full shipped bra/briefs, coverage observations and source weights/masks. Evaluate their surfaces with the body and outer garment. No local foundation offset/weight/mask changes. |
| Knit top | Author a new complete source shape for the stated silhouette, including shoulder/underarm/neck/wrist/hem ownership and source-directed correspondence. Own material/UV/seam work; no inherited graphic or legacy shader as a finished Sugata material. |
| Trousers and boots | Retain accepted navy denim and current boots as controls initially. Explicitly separate trousers from the combined source if used; do not silently substitute its unfitted denim for the accepted runtime garment. Lower-leg/boot relationship remains visible. |
| Exposure | Foundation remains available when the top is removed. In the complete outfit, foundation should be concealed at the neckline/hem/underarms; exposed neck and hands remain body. Proposed visibility changes cannot replace contact/coverage work. |

Before any builder, define the actual editable garment source and correspondence, capture any missing bind/mesh transforms, and give the whole body/foundation/top/trouser combination one consistent pose/appearance contract. Preserve the September 30 full-body and independent coverage requirements, including the 110 required and 3,780 supplementary foundation observations. Source-rest clearance and successful loading are insufficient.

Finite qualification must include source rest, the four retained runtime poses at 0/4/8/12 seconds, front/rear/obliques, exposed foundation and complete outfit, plus one predeclared moving sequence. Use full-surface body/foundation/garment contact and independent coverage, refuse nonfinite or missing correspondence, and inspect actual exported/rendered bytes. Retain visible controls and failures. Numbers support actual appearance review. Stop at incompatible placement or an unowned surface rather than cycling local fixes; the old limited contact witnesses are not a complete inventory.

Current primary [MakeClothes guidance](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb/docs/assets/creating_clothes.html) describes editable clothing meshes and helper templates; [the project's authoring documentation](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/blob/master/docs/ui/create_assets/makeclothes.md) distinguishes OBJ, fitting/weight metadata and materials. These describe the route, not proof the local toolchain currently imports or builds this prototype. The historically recorded Blender data directory is absent at its stated path. Do not extend this source check into installation/recovery.

Alternative A is this own-authored silhouette with complete outfit responsibilities: real wardrobe progress, with substantial coupled source and visual work still required. Alternative B is a supported g050 **calm attentive listener** study using the existing gaze-hold cue: sooner human-readable curation, without new garment/body range, expression infrastructure or intent-recognition claims. Choose A for the responsibility document only; any next construction or curation experiment needs a fresh critic worth-doing check, bounded plan and stop.
