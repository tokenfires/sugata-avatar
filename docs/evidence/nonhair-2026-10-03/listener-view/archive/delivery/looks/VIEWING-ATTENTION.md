# Try a brief attention view

Use the Sage/lavender Portrait look to try the avatar's existing neutral attention action.

1. Open the [Wardrobe study](http://127.0.0.1:5173/src/showcase.html), choose **Open settings (JSON) ↑**, and select [sage-lavender-chin.json](sage-lavender-chin.json).
2. Wait for the look to load. It opens in Portrait with Neutral Studio light.
3. Scroll to **Attention · experimental**. If the page says **Resume motion to try attention**, choose **Resume motion** first.
4. Choose **Look toward me** for a brief neutral attention action toward the current camera. Keep the initial view while trying it.
5. Choose **Release** while the action is active to resume ordinary live motion. The head continues from its current pose. The action also eases out automatically after a few seconds. Turning the view or pausing motion ends it.

In the recorded example, the starting sideward head pose obscures one eye behind the hair. The attention action brings the face nearer the camera and makes both eyes easier to see. After Release, the control returns to idle while the head remains more frontal than the starting sample.
