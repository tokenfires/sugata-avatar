# Independent value of a whole-front modelling blockout

Recommendation: **one at-most-20-minute isolated artistic blockout can be worth constructing**, provided its literal geometry and head attachment are declared before construction. It must be a new preview-owned rigid modelling volume, not an extension of the accepted production groom. The usable output is an editable source parameter file/generator plus an actual registered front/oblique comparison, retained as an accepted or refused design proposal. It is not a finished hairstyle, fit/motion/strand/shading pass, or runtime/asset promotion.

The decision it can change is concrete: does one coherent crown/part/side mass provide a better direction for continuous front flow and open-face framing than the current separated coarse strips, while keeping the jaw-level ends and both eyes legible? This supplies a 3D modelling target rather than another summary or sparse guide drawing. It does not infer a final scalp coverage domain or physical guide assignment.

## Smallest useful construction contract

Before running anything, declare one fixed source-owned coarse **whole-front** proposal: crown mass, part groove/intended side, continuous side masses, forehead opening and jaw-level ends, with literal control geometry and finite extents. State which masses are genuinely joined and which are intentionally divided at the part. A sphere/helmet that simply covers the old strips cannot satisfy the stated flow objective. No reference-side choice may be inferred from the failed guide target.

Own the display role explicitly outside the production groom loader/solver: a separate modelling preview can use a deliberately rigid head attachment, with the existing resolved head bone and recorded source/rest→head-local→world transforms. Do not guess a mesh named body or reinterpret source/helper IDs as runtime IDs. Record what happens to the original groom's visibility in each comparison; keep its bytes/default and body/wardrobe/light/camera configuration preserved. Use a labelled modelling material, and judge shape rather than production hair colour, strand alpha or motion.

Set the total budget to20minutes including declaration, construction, one finite native front+oblique packet and root/critic review. Allow at most5minutes for the literal proposal/ownership admission. Stop before construction if attachment or geometry remains unspecified; stop after the sole packet if the volume is unreviewable, hides the eyes/face, gives an unwanted helmet/ear bulge, lacks crown-to-ends continuity, or changes no useful design decision. Retain the source and actual failures. No adjustment loop, asset adoption or construction of a production groom follows.

## Two alternatives and tradeoffs

**A — isolated modelling proposal:** own a new rigid whole-front volume as described and compare its shape in registered native views. This can deliver an editable 3D design target and advance the missing whole-front artistic decision. It has limited strand/material realism and does not supply dynamic guide mapping or scalp coverage proof. Prefer A only when its explicit new preview responsibility can be stated immediately.

**B — defer new hair geometry:** retain the accepted recipe and the now-delivered attention viewing guide as the usable current character presentation. Select a separately witnessed wardrobe/character usability issue later; none is invented by this value check. This avoids another underspecified geometry or inventory chain, but contributes no new hair authoring target. If A's first5minutes only restate missing mapping/variables, choose B and end the question rather than extend the brief.

This conditional recommendation does not reduce the full AAA/representation or own-wardrobe ambition. It separates coarse artistic shape value from the later complete source, guide, material, contact, coverage, motion and appearance qualification.
