# Open overshirt: proposed garment responsibilities

This brief describes an artistic target, pending the single image review. It does not authorize construction or qualify fitting, contact, motion, or materials.

Postflight: retain this written direction only. The single raster was refused under context preservation and has shorter rolled sleeves than this elbow-length target. It is not the complete accepted garment specification. Root and critic independently reviewed actual sources/output; see their separate decisions.

The proposed outer layer is a separate light cotton-twill garment. Its two open front edges reveal the existing sage tee; the pointed collar belongs to the outer garment, not a replacement tee neckline. One patch pocket, elbow-length rolled cuffs, and a straight upper-hip hem establish the casual silhouette. Denim and boots remain separate existing items.

Before any builder, a new contract must define outer and inner surfaces, thickness and seam ownership; which body and tee regions remain visible or are hidden; a source-directed correspondence for skinning; contact responsibilities at shoulders, underarms, collar, plackets and hem; and a representative body and motion range. An illustration cannot supply those entities or prove them.

The design decision is whether these visible relationships form a coherent, useful outfit direction while preserving the actual character and scene. If the generated image substitutes the character, repairs existing hair or neckline, changes pose or obscures key layer boundaries, refuse it under this packet rather than deriving geometry from it.

The alternative is a separately authored low mock-neck single tee. It has fewer interactions between garments but concentrates the fit problem at the neckline and adds less style variety. Neither option reopens the closed foundation or hair tuning routes.
