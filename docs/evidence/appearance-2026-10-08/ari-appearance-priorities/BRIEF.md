## Why wardrobe responsibility comes next

**Witness:** the concept's rust jacket, gray top and broad trouser framing differ visibly from the bare native control; the bomber sheet supplies legible original opening/collar/trim roles. **Implication:** there is a distinct character-design responsibility to settle without repeating a closed face or waist parameter trial.

A: own an original short-bomber wardrobe source against the unchanged baseline. This can develop recognizable outfit vocabulary while keeping body uncertainty visible. It costs topology, material, inside-edge, coverage and body/rig/morph/pose coupling work; the drawing cannot supply those proofs.

B: independently own a new body/form source direction first. It could address underlying silhouette more directly, but requires complete correspondence, auxiliary refit, rest/rig/expression responsibility and a reference strategy that separates clothing, expression, angle and light. It cannot revive the closed three-control waist recipe.

Choose A as the next **source question**, with B retained if its responsibilities cannot be owned. This does not accept the dressed concept as anatomy or authorize an asset, coordinate recipe, fit solver, coefficient or native job.

## The next bounded question and stop

Can one explicit original-bomber authoring contract own the source and coupling needed for a first static wardrobe comparison on the exact current Ari baseline?

Before any construction, that separate contract must identify:

- Original authorship/provenance and exact body/native/geometry identity; the SVG remains a design reference, not a body-registered pattern.
- Complete garment topology and material roles, intended interior/inside edges, collar/opening/trim responsibilities and deliberate coverage/exposure. Do not substitute an unregistered prior garment or foundation source for Ari authority.
- Body and helper preservation, any underlayer/foundation source responsibility, rig/rest/morph/pose coupling and explicit fit/contact predicates. Static coverage and body range must be distinguished from motion acceptance.
- Frozen neutral controls and complete first-view framing; actual originals and independent visual review. Keep the bare baseline visible and source unchanged.
- Explicit limits for physical cloth/sewing/closure, body ranges, motion, normals/material/export/runtime and performance. A prototype may have scoped limits, but the broader goals stay open.

**Stop at the first unowned source, correspondence, interior, coverage, coupling or acceptance responsibility before code or geometry.** No asset hunt, source lookup, garment programme or experiment is admitted by this brief. A future source inquiry needs its own finite inputs, decision and critic worth; do not expand the search if the nominated evidence cannot answer it.
