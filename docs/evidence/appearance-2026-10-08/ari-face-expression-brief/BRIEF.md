# Ari face brief

The retained concept is a fictional character reference. Its portrait/front show a restrained closed-lip smile, soft cheek/lower-face framing and clear brow/eye presentation. Current head-front/profile are controls at different angles and lighting with a different resting mouth/corner presentation. These images support a qualitative feature discussion; they do not measure neutral anatomy or justify sculpting.

First question: separate a gentle expression from underlying form. A smile study would aim for relaxed mouth corners, readable lips and eyes, and coherent cheek/ear framing while preserving the frozen base geometry responsibilities. The concept’s cheek fullness may partly reflect expression, view and light. Exact facial proportions, identity correspondence and fine-skin finish remain unqualified.

Existing source candidates: mouthSmileLeft and mouthSmileRight are each recorded on Human, Human.eyebrow001 and Human.eyelashes01, all at0.0. Neither exact name appears on Human.high-poly (eyes), Human.teeth_base or Human.tongue01. This is name membership only: absence does not prove those owners should have no effect or that support is missing. It does not prove an expression, geometric delta, synchronization, driver relationship, physical mouth/skin clearance or safe control application.

A preferred next nomination: one separately contracted readonly source probe of these two exact candidates and all6 owners, exposing raw relative-key delta/support, finite data and animation/driver/helper associations. Stop first unowned association before coefficients/setters/render. This can decide whether an existing gentle-smile route is owned well enough to propose a first image study. It avoids sculpting from a confounded expression reference, but cannot itself validate the smile visually.
