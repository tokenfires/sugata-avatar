What the first groom must show

  • Connected, compact crown with readable curl groups and finer strands.
  • Clear forehead and ears, short sides and nape, restrained asymmetry.
  • No detached roots, ear-shaped side lumps, flat helmet shape or regular wire-like repetition.

The authoring choice is editable native curve paths. Before construction, the study needs a declared scalp region and checks that include every new curl. Face, skin, body balance, wardrobe, expression and moving-avatar quality remain unfinished.
