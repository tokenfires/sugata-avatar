# Independent foundation-band feasibility critique

## Disposition

**The declared source coverage prerequisites pass. The complete-below-cut subset is not a construction-ready garment.** The original 110 chest-target rays retain their exact first hits, and the additional baseline-relative fan prerequisite loses no covered sample. However, whole-face selection introduces thirteen disconnected vertex links. A real mixed edge also needs five nonzero skin influences under ordinary interpolation.

Recommend one separately declared **native band-only foundation prototype**, using the existing anatomical band formula and existing hem authoring, with accepted assets unchanged. This is supported as a next construction question, not as appearance, clearance, skinning or moving-coverage acceptance.

## Finding 1: the thirteen subset pinches are introduced

The full original bra has **11,979 valid vertex links: 10,107 cycles and 1,872 paths, zero invalid links**. Its four boundary loops have 400, 680, 392 and 400 vertices. Independent reconstruction of the below subset gives 5,716 cycle links, 903 path links and **13 disconnected links**. All thirteen are new under the whole-face selection.

Concrete witness: welded group 9573 is raw vertex 9618 at `(-0.1045747250, 1.2689819336, 0.1255849898)` m. The subset keeps faces 4704, 4705 and 21287, giving two disconnected link components. The original star also contains mixed faces 15488, 15491, 21278 and 21286, which connect it correctly. The subset's boundary graph has one 392-vertex component and one 524-vertex component; the latter contains **thirteen degree-4 vertices**. Its Euler 0 and zero nonmanifold-edge count therefore do not establish a valid annulus.

Two concrete construction directions:

- **A — recommended: author a fresh band-only native region.** Keep the anatomical band formula from [build_figure.py](../../../../tools/figure-pipeline/build_figure.py), lines 1038–1054, but omit the shoulder-strap branch in an isolated construction. Reuse the established region refinement, offset/relaxation and `roll_the_hem` stages, lines 1346–1384. This supplies a deliberate new rim and avoids treating the conservative face subset as a finished mesh. It changes more geometry than a subset operation and needs its own topology, native/export binding, coverage, appearance and motion receipts.
- **B — preserve original vertices through an existing-edge boundary.** Find one simple upper boundary that preserves the original required coverage witnesses and valid links, while retaining the lower hem and original four-weight vertices. This reduces interpolation and runtime representation changes, but may produce an irregular contour. No suitable loop has yet been established, and adding faces solely until topology passes would need an explicit selection rule and coverage/appearance limits.

Do not repair the thirteen links by arbitrary face deletion or assume a rolling operation will cure invalid input topology.

Mixed face 6636, raw edge **4121→1200**, crosses the plane at fraction **0.7503045303687784**. Its cut point is `(0.0765574793, 1.2695089003, -0.0770430932)` m. The endpoint rows have four slots each, but their positive union is five bones:

- **A — preserve the complete influence union in an isolated clipping implementation.** This avoids automatic four-slot truncation, but needs an explicitly supported storage/skin path and native posed comparison. Even full interpolated weights do not by themselves prove that a newly interpolated rest vertex follows the original moving edge exactly; that relationship needs measurement.
- **B — retain the current four-slot representation deliberately.** Use an existing-edge boundary, or make any reduction/rebinding explicit in the native band prototype and compare against the unreduced native result under unchanged gates. This keeps current renderer integration smaller, but accepts no silent approximation: a failed native comparison stays failed.

The fresh native band route does not automatically resolve this issue. Its exported weights must be inspected and compared rather than assuming that reuse of the exporter proves binding fidelity.

## Boundary and scope of the next prototype

The reported minimum nearest-body distance, **0.727399 mm**, concerns sampled mixed-edge cut points only. It does not qualify a new hem, full triangles, volume containment or a moving garment. Likewise, covering all finite normal rays does not establish front-face winding or visibility from every view.

A separate native-band contract should therefore state the fixed band-only rule, protected independent chest targets, unchanged body/outer masks, new foundation-mask handling, intended hem stages and exact native/export skin comparison before construction. It should retain the original foundation as the matched control and require actual foundation-only and dressed images before any appearance disposition. The existing 110-vertex gate and the separately chosen no-new-fan-loss prerequisite remain distinct.

The observed source coverage supports that bounded next step. It does not authorize a cutoff-height sweep, promotion of this invalid subset, or replacement of the accepted foundation.
