1. **A — separate pose/binding diagnosis from outfit visibility, starting with the already measured body-contact witness.** Reproduce the exact saved runtime band/body geometry from bound inputs before attributing a mechanism, and carry this packet's band/blouse ownership into any subsequent fit contract. This answers why passing source-rest clearance did not survive the relaxed-standing runtime pose; it does not itself repair the blouse or prove that an offset/weight change is appropriate. This is the selected bounded direction.
2. **B — declare a separate outfit-aware rendering contract before any mask calibration.** Preserve the known geometric failures and explicitly establish which foundation regions may be hidden under elegant clothing using independent coverage and moving/outfit witnesses. This could address the visible contribution while leaving geometry unchanged, but cannot convert the failed full-body exterior gate into success and risks new coverage gaps if based only on these two image windows. No mask change is authorized by this evidence alone.
