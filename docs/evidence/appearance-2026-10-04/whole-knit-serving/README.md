[Quad OBJ](sweater.obj) and [mesh JSON](mesh.json) are editable sources; the original [author and saved operands](../whole-knit-resume/RECEIPT.md) retain their construction history.

Root and the independent critic actually inspected all three unchanged original frames. They retain this continuous torso/sleeve volume as a useful **unitless artist scaffold**. It is a simple T-rest shape, with broad planar torso areas and minimal neck shaping. Physical dimensions, relaxed worn proportions, dropped seams, rib/fold/knit finish, body fit, rig and motion remain open. It is separate from the flat-piece sheet; no physical folding or pattern-to-volume correspondence is established.
