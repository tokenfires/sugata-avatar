# A distinct masculine starter target — proposed artist contract

Original conceptual art only: one attractive adult character with an angular but soft face, warm medium-brown skin, short dark swept hair, and a lightly structured olive overshirt worn open over an ivory crew tee. Sandstone straight trousers and brown leather boots complete the outfit. These are Cora's authored design choices for a new discussion target, pending independent worth/reference/prompt review. No existing Rowan identity, body bake, bob asset or costume is copied or altered.

The useful decision is whether a short, lifted crown and neat temple/nape treatment keep both eyes, brows and jaw readable while an open woven layer gives a complete silhouette distinct from the blue-knit/bob target. Masculine styling is the design intention; the illustration cannot certify gender perception or represent all identities. Keep facial softness, natural asymmetry, restrained skin texture and relaxed hands. Do not substitute a caricatured jaw, exaggerated physique or angry expression for that intention.

One landscape image should pair a complete three-quarter body view, head through both boots and hands visible, with a larger three-quarter head/upper-chest portrait of the same character. Stable soft neutral studio light and matching face, hair, collar, tee and material colours make the concept discussable. The portrait should expose a complete crown, both eyes/brows, temple, ear and jaw. Short hair needs continuous crown-to-temple flow, moderate root lift and controlled flyaways; eyes must remain readable. The body silhouette should show the open plackets and distinct inner tee, intentional shoulder and wrist shapes, trouser fall and boot uppers/soles.

Retain only if the actual single unchanged output supplies those visible choices coherently. Missing head/hands/boots, materially conflicting identity or outfit, hidden eyes/jaw or unreadable layer separation means B without editing or regeneration. Minor illustrative differences must be named, not converted into exact geometric correspondence. One primary source example may inform observable styling/material, never hidden topology, exact dimensions or a copied person.

Even an A is an artist reference, not a curated runtime starter. Future work must independently author complete head/groom attachment, skin and material regions, underlying body/foundation, all overshirt/tee/waist/seat/leg/boot interfaces, exposure/coverage and separate garments, rig/morph/pose and moving appearance. Existing hair/foundation/Rowan/capture closures remain. Full AAA, representative bodies/identities, expressions/gaze/gestures, outfits, performance and later VTuber/Mii work stay open.
