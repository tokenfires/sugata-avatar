ARTIST-DATA.json owns every row and cross-section sample. The producer expands only those samples: x=centre+halfWidth×shape.x, z=frontDepth×shape.depth or −backDepth×shape.depth, y=row.y. One export/check is admitted only after complete critic and root source review. The producer records partial state before each phase, rejects at the first failed gate and never repairs geometry. It checks finite coordinates, nonzero triangles, opposite internal edge directions, one connected face component, exact three named boundary cycles, connected vertex links and literal expected counts/Euler characteristic. These checks do not certify global self-intersection, body enclosure, clearance, pattern feasibility or fit.

The external runner pins exact inputs/scripts, records its own and child PID, wall time, exit/timeout and all output byte hashes. It permits ten seconds, then TERM/two seconds/KILL/two seconds/reap. A marker forbids repeating the attempt. No subprocess is spawned by the producer.
