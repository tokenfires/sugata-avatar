# Relaxed wide-trouser design

**Retained artist target, October 4.** One garment-only sheet gives the wider-trouser direction a deliberately chosen profile and rear. It extends the [whole-character front concept](KNIT-CHARACTER-TARGET-2026-10-04.md) while preserving charcoal cloth and warm brown boots.

A simple waistband, front fly and discreet front pockets provide a quiet tailored finish. Small hem folds give a controlled break over the boots, with readable toes, side heels and rear soles. These choices replace previously undefined side/rear styling with a concrete discussion target.
