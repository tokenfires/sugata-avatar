attachments match, but temporal resolve differs. This new pair asks whether the two views
already have different inherited history immediately before their final image draw. It does
not repeat the prior pair to seek equality or change an acceptance threshold.

uniform/matrix owners and every observed value must remain exact throughout the serial reads

qualified uncompressed colour formats, not depth copy/map semantics. Record the exact depth

the final observed result differs; or this pair may match. Collect both controls, preserve any

timing, performance, or an uninstrumented image. Retain all errors and failures; no retries to
obtain equality. Depth, earlier frames and GPU uniform contents remain explicit missing inputs.
