# One face primitive before a starter-look library

The next substantive option is an isolated, creation-time **nose tip width** primitive using the existing `nose/nose-point-width-decr-incr` source target. This is a proposal for qualification, not an implemented Avatar option or an approved appearance/range. No product, accepted asset, dependency or hair source was changed; no browser or GPU job was run.

A three-card outfit/palette shelf would mostly duplicate the six current Compare palettes actions. I do not recommend spending the next slice on that shelf. Existing choices already restore two complete outfits and three palettes, including either outfit under either accepted cut. “Mixed combination” does not mean a separately interchangeable tee, skirt or trousers: both suit assets occupy TORSO, ARMS, HIPS and LEGS and conflict at the same layer. The larger goal of handsome male, beautiful female and deliberate gender-neutral starter builds remains open; three settings on g050 would not meet it.

The two current starting cards principally choose a cut plus outfit, not a fully authored new identity. `showcase.js:128` preserves palette and scene when those cards navigate, but drops framing and studio-light selection; a future recipe should not copy that behavior by accident.
- `packages/core/src/figure/Identity.js:88` explicitly stores age/build/height as no-ops. Gender chooses a whole body/skeleton bake, not a head-only setting. `Avatar.js:4653` rejects wardrobe plans other than the one resolved g050 bake. No current Avatar identity entry point applies the diagnostic face-target values.
- `packages/testbed/src/identity.js:59` has “angular face” and “soft oval” diagnostic stacks; `identity.html:111` explicitly warns that fitted eyes, cornea, teeth, tongue, brows/lashes and skeleton are not updated. These are not qualified character presets. `IdentityTargets` arithmetic is reusable; the diagnostic page is not a complete implementation.
- `showcase-settings.mjs:5` accepts only the exact reconstructed supported configuration; `showcase-collection.mjs:67` uses that same schema. A read-only CPU check reproduced all three proposed outfit/palette combinations under both existing cuts through JSON, URL and an in-memory named collection. Added `identity.targets`, `identity.age`, `expression`, and both full suits together were correctly rejected. This check proved serialization behavior only, and is not an argument for adding duplicate recipe UI.
- Wardrobe attention actions are transient, explicitly experimental and not a saved facial identity. A new face variant needs its own truthful actual-state/readback and persistence contract after the primitive is qualified; adding a label to the current config cannot supply that behavior.

## Current source support proof

[Inventory v4](inventory-v4.json) is the current receipt, generated by [inventory.py](inventory.py). It binds the actual body/bare GLBs, identity catalogue/map/nose bin, five source face-fit rules, both suit rules, shoes rules, and their cached archive bytes. It inventories all **42 authored nose-target ends**, not an amplitude search. The current wardrobe and bare g050 bodies have exactly equal POSITION and all **89** actual POSITION morph arrays across **14,517** positions. The existing source map covers all 13,380 unique body indices, with exact split-copy position agreement.

The independent readout of packed target records was checked against the actual `IdentityCatalogue.resolve` and `IdentityTargets.apply`: all 42 support sets and maximum unit displacements agree exactly. “Unit” here is the catalogue's authored endpoint, not an approved product setting or an attractiveness claim.

For both ends:

- Every affected body vertex has only the existing `head` skin influence, weight 1.
- No affected source index belongs to any retained joint/helper group, including eye, mouth, jaw, teeth, tongue or hair helpers.
- None intersects **any** of the five current fitted face-part source references or their scale references. None intersects the two full-suit or shoes fitting references/scales. Therefore this delta changes no input to those source fitters; no helper baseline reconstruction or native refit result is being substituted.
- Neither the moved vertices nor the complete incident-triangle vertex set intersects any current `eye*` morph's nonzero support. Every current garment hide and decency mask is zero on the moved support. No masks are changed.
- Actual support is confined to a nose-tip box. Decrease: x ±14.8580 mm, y 1.5108179–1.5281738 m, z 0.1482320–0.1592235 m. Increase: x ±12.0445 mm, y 1.5128158–1.5252004 m, z 0.1503165–0.1592235 m. These are source mesh coordinates, not guessed anatomical segmentation.

The bigger “nose width” slider is less isolated: its decrease end intersects 11 blink-support vertices per side, while its increase also intersects a fitted-part dependency. Tip width is preferable because the support proof is narrower, not because its appearance has been judged better.

## What that does and does not establish

It is feasible to qualify this one primitive without first implementing all body/garment refitting or all generic identity correctives. The cached-native helper oracle gap does not block an input-invariance proof where every relevant source dependency is unchanged.

This does **not** prove that the new nose looks good, remains well-shaped during every expression, or is ready for a starter build. The moved nose vertices overlap mouth/jaw, noseSneer and speech morph supports. That overlap is expected facial coupling, not proof of a defect; zero additive superposition error is also not proof of a correct resulting shape. Exact mouth/lid-margin topology and rendered views remain necessary. Root's separate actual-triangle eyelid aperture work under `identity-closure-next/` is the appropriate eye gate, including its failing control.

Normal ownership is a concrete unresolved implementation detail: the current body has an authored NORMAL attribute and only POSITION morph targets. `identity.js` updates positions and a bounding sphere; it does not make a new normal field. A creation-time appearance candidate must declare and test how affected normals are produced, with exact zero-change parity and no unrelated whole-body re-normalization. Unchanged UVs and maps preserve resource ownership, but the curvature/cavity maps were baked for the original geometry; unchanged map bytes alone do not qualify their appearance on the modified tip. Do not hide these issues behind a flattering light.

The retained group table has a `helper-hair` group, but not the actual `scalp` attachment group read by the hair authoring code. Its zero intersection is **not** a complete groom attachment or clearance proof. Keep the accepted groom unchanged, verify the final local geometry remains away from its roots/visible strands, and do not turn that check into hair reauthoring. Facial proxy invariance is likewise a statement about exact declared source dependencies; the future native run must verify the actual seven meshes and their transforms.

## Smallest next experiment

1. **Finish source/geometry admission first.** Bind current source and a single named tip-width target. Prove zero-weight identity byte parity for body attributes, all morph arrays, all other mesh geometries, source masks, bones and resources. Keep each moved triangle and its full normal neighborhood explicit. Check no triangle degeneration/self-intersection at the declared source endpoints, and actual triangle-level lid/mouth/proxy separation rather than nearest-vertex or vertical-mean proxies. The existing endpoint values can bound a diagnostic; no aesthetic operating range is selected here.
2. **Use a fresh, isolated creation path.** A capture-only wrapper around the exact `Figure.load` result can apply to the newly owned body before `Avatar.swapFigure` builds skin, wardrobe, skeleton/rest/motion bindings (`Avatar.js:3014–3022`). No live-pose mutation, public generic identity API, asset export, callback overwrite or material/lighting change. Assert one expected source figure and instance; retire that owner on a post-load failure. Root must approve the exact module and normal policy before implementation.
3. **Only then run a bounded native comparison.** Fresh unchanged baseline and the agreed fixed target case(s), same g050/outfit/cut/seed/camera/scene. Preserve all nonselected geometry, rig/rest/binding state and native owner outputs exactly where source predicts equality; retain any differences instead of broad exemptions. Check neutral, full native blink, jaw open/close, pucker, smile/nose-sneer and actual speech. Use front, three-quarter and profile portrait views plus one ordinary clothed view in Studio and Kitchen. This is one primitive qualification, not all-motion, wider-body fit or an attractiveness/representation verdict.
4. **A UI/config change comes after that result.** If admitted, the useful user-facing change is an actual persistent face choice with an explicit target-library binding, applied before initialization and reported from the attached identity. If failed, preserve the precise locality/normal/closure witness and address that primitive; do not expose a decorative slider or relabel the body.

The alternative is immediate full diagnostic “angular/soft oval” integration. It offers more visible distinction but combines eye, chin, cheek, forehead and mouth dependencies plus unresolved proxy/corrective ownership. That is not the smallest credible step within this workday. The tip-width primitive trades dramatic difference for a tractable, real face-identity capability that can become part of later complete starter builds.
