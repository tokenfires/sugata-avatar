# Ari interior: reference observations

Two searches and two primary pages, retrieved October 18 Pacific. The research budget is complete. Full returned text and metadata are preserved in PRIMARY-CLARE.json and PRIMARY-BARACUTA.json; [receipt](REFERENCES-RECEIPT.json) records exact sources, capture identities and limits.

[Closet Core's Clare lining and facing article](https://blog.closetcorepatterns.com/clare-sewalong-sewing-the-lining-facing/) describes separate back/sleeve lining, a center-back pleat, neck/front facing assembly and collar attachment. Its [actual photograph 23](https://blog.closetcorepatterns.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/Clare-Coat-sewalong_Sewing-coat-lining-and-facing-23.jpg), inspected in [the preserved browser view](reference-clare.jpg), visibly distinguishes blue lining from a contrasting neck/front facing and shows both sleeves. The back pleat is a textual reference, not established by this single photo. The coat's asymmetric front, raglan construction, measurements and sewing sequence are not Ari's design.

[Baracuta's G9 product page](https://us.baracuta.com/products/baracuta-g9-harrington-jacket-brcps3001ut3124-1007) names a contrasting lining and a separate internal pocket. The [selected photo 5](https://us.baracuta.com/cdn/shop/files/BRCPS3001UT3124_1007_BRC_5.jpg?v=1784542814), actually inspected in [the preserved browser view](reference-baracuta.jpg), is a portrait with narrow red inner-edge strips at the opened front. It does **not** show the complete lining, pocket bag or sleeve interfaces. Only visible inner-edge contrast informs this study. No internal pocket, tartan, button collar, raglan seam or branded feature is being added to Ari.

Ari's warm pale lining, rust exterior, pointed shirt collar, two slanted outer pocket mouths and their hidden bags remain the retained original choices. The new back/sleeve lining, neck facing, center-back fold symbol and explicit connection map will be original unitless artist intent. References supply vocabulary, not a cut pattern, physical material specification or fitted garment. No reference artwork is traced or incorporated into the new vectors.
