# Trouser reference observations

Retrieved October19UTC/October18Pacific with two Firecrawl searches and two live page requests (`maxAge:0`, HTTP200). Exactly two root-owned primary image views are saved. No pattern was bought, traced or imported.

[Closet Core: Introducing the Mitchell Trousers](https://blog.closetcorepatterns.com/introducing-the-mitchell-trousers/) (2022 article, current retrieval). The text distinguishes a faced waist without a separate waistband, anchored front pleats integrating with angled pockets, a fly front and two leg shapes. Root viewed its [technical flat](https://blog.closetcorepatterns.com//wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Mitchell-Trousers-Pattern_Technical-Flat.jpg): front/rear tapered and wider versions, long front fold lines, fly marking and optional rear pocket/tie details. The drawing supplies visible design vocabulary, not Ari's proportions or a transferable cut pattern. Its hidden facing is supported by article text, not the flat's pixels. Ari will have a newly authored separate band and independently placed pocket mouths, so this is not the Mitchell construction.

[Helen's Closet: Winslow elastic-back study](https://helensclosetpatterns.com/blogs/helens-closet/winslow-culottes-hack-elastic-back) (current retrieval). The text retains front pleats while changing the back waist and removing the original zipper. Root viewed the supplied [waist close-up](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0715/6701/6159/files/half_elastic_horizontal_2.jpg): a smooth front band meets a gathered side/back segment above visible front pleats. It supports the visible distinction between front and rear treatment. It does not show a fly, prove stretch range, or supply Ari's hidden assembly. Ari's proposed fly is a deliberate new artist requirement, not a claim that this reference includes one.

Decision: retain a calm front, explicit pleat and fly roles, and limited rear-band gathering as original artist intent. Continuous front elastic/gathers would change the selected front reading. Actual comfort, pull-on access, closure mechanics, elastic range, sewing compatibility, body fit and moving cloth remain unqualified. The existing original character supplies only silhouette/palette context. Two searches/pages/primary views are exhausted; no further reference hunt.
