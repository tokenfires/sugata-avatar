# Independent critique — two-card guide-clearance probe

## Disposition

**The source mechanism is demonstrated; the visible improvement is marginal. Preserve this diagnostic and reject adoption as a groom improvement. Stop further local passes on these two cards.**

Removing guide clearance from cards 322 and 449, while retaining their original blend, original ribbon construction and final vertex clamp, substantially reduces their sharp centreline turns. The final selected triangles also pass the reported independent rest body-intersection check. The actual images show a small rearrangement in the lower rear patch, but do not establish the coherent crown-to-tip flow requested in the medium-bob brief.

Root selected alternative A below: one separately frozen posterior support-envelope prototype using existing cards. The prototype is not combined with the successful nape/distal lead. Production assets remain the control.

Reviewed candidate SHA-256: `32e767725cf9c1f3e752e906d5f481fd0f0db03720575c1eb1a31762f0786da9`, independently rehashed by the critic.

It still invokes the original function, so the original clump blend remains. Production `draw_into_lock` returns that blend before its body-projection loop when `body is None`; the separate final card-vertex clamp remains enabled. Original card order, RNG consumption and ribbon construction are retained. The build records 496 draws.

The [isolation script](../../../../captures/bob-clump-clearance-v1/isolate-bob-clump-clearance.mjs) checks rebuilt positions/normals outside the selected cards against the original fixture, checks both UV sets and skin attributes, then transplants only the selected source outputs onto the accepted post-export asset. It does not support the stronger conclusion that guide clearance caused the entire visible rear disorder.

## Two concrete alternatives

### A — selected: one posterior support-envelope prototype

Reshape existing rooted posterior paths around a shared smooth support envelope. Include exposed posterior short-root paths as well as the outer cards that contribute to the rear patch. Establish coherent crown-to-hem directions with outward body and inward tips, then transport the ribbon frame consistently along those paths.

**Why this is supported:** the source trace identifies several distinct contributors. Card 322's sharp turn enters through guide clearance; card 276 and root cards 24/40 acquire substantial turns in final vertex clamping; card 281 already develops its strong bend during clump blending. The native images retain lateral crossings after the two-card guide-clearance contribution is removed. A representation that coordinates neighboring paths addresses more of this observed problem than another local endpoint or width-axis adjustment.

**Scope for the first prototype:**

- Use existing cards, count, topology, material, and width magnitudes. Do not add a rigid curtain over the original disorder.
- Declare the exact posterior membership and protected front boundary before generating the candidate. Root's chosen boundary is an explicit source root plane. Preserve authored geometry on the protected side and retain a manifest of changed cards/vertices.
- Keep the scalp cap and front/face assets as controls. If the selected field reaches the protected boundary, document that transition and inspect its silhouette; a root-plane selection alone does not prove that no selected shaft projects into the face view.
- Retain the original runtime solver and record the resulting shared collider fit. Authored front geometry may remain identical while live front geometry changes through a shared collider parameter. Measure that possibility rather than asserting runtime isolation.
- Keep the nape/distal lead separately frozen. The first prototype should reveal what this representation does without combining successful and experimental recipes.
- Compare intact rear, opposite-rear and profile appearance; retain a front view as a boundary check. Judge connected flow, coverage, silhouette and cut together with rest clearance. A later motion qualification remains separate.

**Tradeoffs:** this is a larger change with its own risks: broad panels, repeated comb-like divisions, slots between locks, excessive uniformity, and boundary seams. The September 13 curtain studies are explicit negative controls: those added 64 cards with 49 rings and produced panels/comb structure. Keeping existing rooted cards avoids repeating that construction, but does not guarantee a natural appearance. Freeze one first prototype and review its actual failure or success before considering another intervention.

### B — alternative: one broader posterior application of the same clearance-stage probe

Apply the demonstrated guide-clearance bypass once to a documented posterior set, retaining original clump blends, ribbon construction and final body clamp, then preserve unrelated accepted geometry through an explicit transplant.

**Why this remains plausible:** sharp turns introduced by guide clearance are widespread in the trace, so the two-card sample may be too small to reveal the aggregate visual benefit. This alternative changes fewer mechanisms than a support-envelope representation and could reduce more local wrinkles while retaining the current cut and authored path field.

**Tradeoffs:** the original lateral guide directions and lock relationships remain. The current two-card images show that smoother individual cards can still participate in a visibly crossed patch. The final clamp can also reintroduce irregularity, and vertex clearance still requires an independent triangle audit. This would be one bounded source-stage experiment, not a sweep over clump/standoff/width parameters. It is less well matched to the larger directional-flow problem, so alternative A is preferred now.

## Stop conditions and limits

- Do not continue local 322/449 adjustments. A new source mechanism was tested, its geometric effect was established, and its visible gain was insufficient.
- Do not call the current probe collision-free in motion. The reported zero intersections cover the selected 64 triangles at rest against the audited body.
- Do not infer that every unchanged-looking pixel is geometrically unchanged, or that the pale triangular region is a proven hole. Those claims require their own evidence.
- Do not promote a broader prototype merely because its angles are smaller or its triangles clear the body. It must visibly improve the medium-bob target without replacing the rear patch with panels, slots, a rigid curtain, or a conspicuous front-boundary seam.

This review used completed source/reports and direct viewing of original images. No native browser, Blender or GPU job was run by the critic. Only this assigned critique file was written for this disposition.
