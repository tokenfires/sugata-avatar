## Decision and new question

**Reject `posterior-cluster-v1` for appearance; preserve its exact bytes.** Do not tune these endpoints again. Card276 retains widths of 24.95–35.46 mm while its arc shrinks from 65.75 to 29.94 mm. Card449 retains a 69.84° width-axis change between rings 2 and 3 despite its smoother centreline. Four broad tips also converge into a narrow projected band. Centreline smoothness alone is insufficient.

Selected next probe: freeze the rejected candidate's paths and width magnitudes and change only card449's transverse frame, using smooth transport from its root. This tests a specific folding mechanism; it cannot fix the cluster's coverage design. The alternative is to design a small coverage patch with widths and spacing considered together, a larger experiment that must avoid the earlier flat curtain and repeated teeth. The successful separate card281 nape candidate remains unchanged.
