## Diagnosis and decision

A layer-identity rendering assigns the long near-side slash to the veil group. Hiding only card 382 removes the slash and a front-side triangular protrusion while leaving the smaller upper-ear crossed patch. The [ablation controls](../../../../captures/bob-side-attribution-v1/ablation-controls.json) show exact fixed-state/physical arrays and only 32 render triangles removed.

The original authoring trace shows that clearance projection creates a sharp upper bend on 382, but its rings 4–16 already match before and after that stage. Bypassing the stage would leave most of the offending path. The critic recommended a bounded continuation of the useful rear field. A one-card supported replacement was the alternative; it would leave the adjacent crossed patch and risk another sequence of isolated fixes.

The new authoring region contains 72 nonfringe cards whose original roots have z between 0.015 and 0.060 m and absolute x at least 0.045 m. This is a region for coherent construction, not 72 separately demonstrated defects. Its [root/projection inventory](../../../../captures/bob-side-attribution-v1/side-sector-inventory-v2.json) precedes generation. The first inventory's projected coordinates used a wrong flat-array stride; that output and its rejection are preserved. Root coordinates and selected IDs were unaffected.

It retains root centres, width magnitudes, material, topology, UVs and weights. Posterior-v3 coordinates remain exact. No material, fringe or hem parameter is tuned alongside the side region.
