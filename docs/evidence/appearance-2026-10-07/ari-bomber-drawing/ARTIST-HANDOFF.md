Zipper engagement and pocket capacity remain unproved.

Wearer's right is image-left in front/inside, image-right in rear. The authored right profile shows the front at image-left, near P-R and Z-R, and only a small partly hidden near-cuff tab; far pocket, zipper and cuff roles are hidden. Front and rear provide clearer cuff-band views. The inside is front panels only, with sleeves and rear lining omitted. Bag envelopes are schematic proposals, not usable cut pieces.

Pale dotted marks mean zip intent; brown dashed marks mean proposed attachment; wavy repeat marks mean gathered trim intent. These are graphic symbols, with no measured elastic ratio, knit gauge, textile, padding or physical folding proof. Coordinates, depth and hidden roles are deliberate original drawing choices, without raster tracing or registered projections.
