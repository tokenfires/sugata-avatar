# Original facial source and fixed visual decision

Root authors every coordinate and connection in head.py; no pre-existing mesh,
image reconstruction, registered identity, supplier or native-avatar API supplies
them. Original Ari concept-v1.png is qualitative front/profile/portrait intent.
Its fuller cheeks, broad rounded jaw, readable eyes, relaxed closed mouth and
profile nose/chin motivate this separate artist head. Hidden anatomy is invented.
Hair is deliberately absent. The earlier curl test's allowed display ellipsoid
is neither imported nor modified. This duty is facial structure, not a new curl
attachment or a material/pose rescue.

## Literal owned mechanism

Canonical space is right-handed: +X transverse right, +Y up, +Z anterior. The
neck opening is y0 and the top y2.30, all unitless. SECTIONS in head.py gives all
11 authored height/width/depth/centre controls. Smooth scalar interpolation and
angular sections own a connected cranial/jaw/neck envelope. It is a new head
surface construction, with no garment source/loft/control search or inherited
source geometry. No optimizer, target-fitting loop, extracted buffer or hidden
master is involved. The exact program is the geometry authority.

The head uses 128 angular columns and89 open rings, one top vertex and two
explicit 8-by-6-cell eye apertures. Unused interior-aperture vertices are removed.
Each aperture's rectangular boundary maps onto an authored ellipse, with28 rim
vertices; its exterior transitions to the surrounding head. The eyelid rim's
depth follows the declared radius0.17 eye sphere with0.008 anterior relief.
EYE controls own centres, widths/heights, sphere depths and logical addresses.
This owns a finite source interface, not anatomical contact or eye mechanics.

Facial relief is integrated into this same surface: nose bridge/tip/two alar
bases and shallow nostril dents; muzzle/philtrum neighbourhood; two lip ridges
and a continuous closed-mouth groove; cheeks, brows and chin. Parameters and
Gaussian widths/amplitudes are literal source values. No feature is a floating
primitive, texture overlay, drawn SVG or painted substitute for shape. The
closed-mouth seam has no topological opening; nostril dents do not claim airways.
Lip/seam face colours only identify relief already present in the mesh.

Two separately authored closed eyeballs contain sclera/iris/pupil face colours.
The ear pinnae each have two disks, an outer closing strip, integrated helix
relief and a shallow concha. They are separate closed parts, with declared root
frames at(+/-0.75,1.23,-0.015). No welding, anatomical attachment or interpart
clearance is claimed. Their relationship must be visible and artistically
coherent in the first whole views; a numeric pass cannot hide a bad attachment.

Eleven named head landmarks retain logical grid addresses, actual vertex IDs
and coordinates. All three opening loops are owned exactly: neck128 and two
eyelid28 loops. Named regions contain actual head vertex IDs, may overlap and
are not a coverage/semantic partition. The source makes all positions and
connectivity editable; landmark JSON/OBJ retain that ownership. There is no
rig, morph, expression calibration, UV map, authored skin texture, hair, body,
foundation, teeth, tongue, physical scale or garment connection.
