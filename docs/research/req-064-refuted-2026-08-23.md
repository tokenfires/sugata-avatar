# REQ-064 — refuted by the measurement it asked for

**Date:** 2026-08-23 · **Registration:** `79c870e`, committed before any arm was rendered ·
**Tool:** `tools/critic/hair-glint.mjs` · **Plates:** `captures/hair-r32-glint`

REQ-064 asked for a light near the view axis so the retroreflective TRT lobe — the only shipped lobe
that multiplies by the hair's own colour — would have a geometry to fire in. It was the last
coloured-lobe candidate after TT closed on 2026-08-22, and the strongest of the three rig requests
by its own evidence.

**It does not fix "muddy", and the reason is not the one anybody expected.**

---

## 1. What was measured, and against what

Mask: `hair-lightpath.mjs`'s gated groom mask — the eroded shipped-minus-bald difference, then cut
against the lobes-off plate at a threshold that sits in a **measured empty band**. 287,321
invertible px inside the eroded mask; **236,792 kept** as pixels `HairMaterial` actually shades
(82.42%), 50,509 resolving to something behind the groom, 32 in the separating band.

Statistic: mean chroma of the **top-decile-luma** hair pixels, in 8-bit code values — the norm of a
pixel's departure from its own grey. Chroma rather than luma because **both prior assessments of
REQ-064 used a brightness metric and correctly found it small**, and
`docs/research/pedestal-look-2026-08-22.md` §3 says why that was the wrong question: *"The value of
a 1.3% term that is the ONLY saturated hair-coloured highlight is not its energy share."*

Two controls ran before any gate was read.

| control | result | what it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| **decoy** — same light, same power, azimuth 180 where `D_TRT` = 2.1357e-15 | on-axis gain 0.1141 codes, decoy gain **−0.0004**, ratio **0.004** | the statistic tracks GEOMETRY, not brightness |
| **drift** — one configuration captured twice under two different query spellings | **0.0000 codes** | the run did not drift; a tenth of a code value is signal |

A drift floor of exactly zero is what makes a 0.1141-code finding reportable at all.

### Both registered gates fail

- 🔴 **GATE 1 — ATTRIBUTION: 1.048× against a registered floor of 2.0×.** The glint's whole
  top-decile chroma gain is **0.1141 codes with TRT present** and **0.1089 with TRT removed**. TRT
  contributes **4.6%** of the effect the light exists to produce.
- 🔴 **GATE 2 — VISIBILITY: 0.1141 codes against a registered floor of 1.0.** Nine times under the
  8-bit quantisation floor. Below one code value the change cannot be seen on the plate at all.
- ⚪ **GATE 3 — COST: deliberately not measured.** The registration requires all three to pass to
  ship. With one and two failed the branch is decided, and measuring the nine-scene calibration cost
  of something already refused is GPU time spent on a foregone conclusion. Stated rather than
  quietly skipped.

## 3. 🎯 Slide 39's fake carries 88.8% of it, and that was predicted

`packages/core/src/render/LightingRig.js` recorded, before any of this:

> **REQ-064's camera-axis light lands on the FLOOR, not on the band.** […] slide 39's
> multiple-scattering fake carries 65.4% of the groom's rise and its whole angular dependence is a
> wrap-around cosine, so a better geometry feeds the fake before it feeds the lobe.

Measured on the same statistic:

| slide 39's fake | the glint buys |
|---|---:|
| ON (shipped) | **0.1141** codes of chroma |
| OFF (`hairscatter=0`) | **0.0127** codes of chroma |

**88.8% of the gain is the fake being handed a better wrap-around cosine.** The prediction was
65.4% and reality is worse. This is the entire reason the gate was registered as an ATTRIBUTION test
rather than a magnitude one — a magnitude threshold would have been cleared by a larger light and
the conclusion would have been wrong.

## 4. ⚠️ The lobe is not absent. It is tiny everywhere — a different finding

The fair objection to the gates is that a **top-decile** statistic could miss a narrow
retroreflective band. That objection cannot be answered by moving the statistic — it was
pre-registered, and moving it after the data is in is renegotiation. It is answered by an additional
measurement, reported separately: TRT's per-pixel chroma contribution across the **whole** gated
mask, as the difference between the `r,trt` and `r` arms with the glint on.

| over 236,792 gated px | |
|---|---:|
| mean chroma contribution | **0.032 codes** |
| best single pixel | **2.12091 codes** |
| pixels reaching the 1.0-code floor | **644 (0.2720%)** |

So the lobe **does** fire, and somewhere it is genuinely visible. It fires on a scattering of
individual pixels rather than on a band. **The decile was never the limit**, and the gates' verdict
survives the strongest challenge available to it.

## 5. 🎯 What would change the answer is the FIBRE, not the rig

The irradiance sweep is the part that closes the question rather than leaving it as "try harder".

| glint E | chroma (codes) | C* | linear luma |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 (shipped) | 34.5879 | 18.2336 | 1.242e-1 |
| 0.05 | 34.7020 | 18.2739 | 1.251e-1 |
| 0.2 | 35.0143 | 18.3776 | 1.280e-1 |
| 0.5 | 35.5487 | 18.5355 | 1.338e-1 |
| 1.0 | 36.2174 | **18.6926** | 1.442e-1 |
| 2.0 | 36.5351 | 18.4692 | 1.670e-1 |

Reaching gate 2's floor needs **E ≈ 2.0** — which is **two thirds of the key's 3.0**. That is not
REQ-064's "small, low-irradiance practical"; it is a second key light, and it would re-open all nine
scenes, the skin and eye gates, and G2, which sits 0.001 inside its bound.

And it does not even buy colour at that point: **C\* PEAKS AT E 1.0 AND FALLS AT E 2.0** (18.6926 →
18.4692) while linear luma climbs 34%. The top of the sweep is washing out, not warming.

The mechanism is arithmetic and it is in the BSDF rather than in the rig. `absorbTRT` is
`pow(colour, 0.8/cosθd)`, and on a `#150F17` fibre that is **0.022** — so the lobe peaks near
0.0014 sr⁻¹ **whatever is pointed at it**. REQ-064's own entry said this in its ⚠️ and it was right;
what it did not say is that the ceiling makes the request unachievable rather than merely modest.

> **A near-axis light does not make black hair coloured. A lighter fibre would, and that is a
> different request.**

## 6. What was established along the way, and stands independently

- 🎯 **R's relative chroma is EXACTLY 0.0000 at every azimuth**, re-derived from
  `hairScatteringValue`, the BSDF's own CPU mirror, at 0°, 12°, 42° and 168°. "R cannot carry colour
  by construction" is now a computation rather than a quotation. TRT's is **0.4981** and **flat**
  across azimuth — the azimuth moves how much of the lobe there is, not what colour it is.
- 🔴 **REQ-064's change clause named the wrong frame.** It asked for the glint at `azimuthDegrees`
  equal to `CAMERA_AZIMUTH_DEGREES`, which is `Avatar.js:233` — the camera's yaw in the CHARACTER
  frame. The rig's azimuth is measured FROM the camera, so the camera axis is **0**, and 12 is 12°
  off it: `D_TRT` is 1.2461 at 0° and 0.85943 at 12°, **31.0% of the peak discarded**.
  `hair-lightpath.mjs:945` is where it became invisible — *"12 is `CAMERA_AZIMUTH_DEGREES`, read
  from the repo rather than picked"*. R26's and `hair.md` §9.4's figures stand; they measured a light
  12° off axis and must be quoted as that.
- 🔴 **REQ-064 as filed was un-shippable for a second, independent reason.** It asked for a fifth
  AREA light. `MAX_AREA_LIGHTS` is 4 and `placements()` throws above it; the budget is 0.9543 ms per
  area light against a 1.870 ms hair budget. It only became measurable at all as a
  `DirectionalLight`.

## 7. Disposition

**REQ-064 → REFUTED.** `GLINT_LIGHTS.portrait` ships at `irradiance: 0` and `buildLights`
constructs nothing at zero, so the shipped frame pays nothing. The table, the placement and the
override path survive so that `?ov=glint.irradiance:0.05` rebuilds the light and every arm above
reproduces — a refutation that cannot be re-run is a paragraph, not a result.

**The coloured-lobe list is now empty.** TT closed 2026-08-22 (geometric: the rim supplies 91.21% of
it and 0.48% of fragments have a clear path). TRT closes here (absorption-limited: 0.022 on this
fibre, whatever is pointed at it). R cannot carry colour by construction, measured at exactly zero.

**So "muddy" is not a lighting problem and it is not a lobe-weighting problem.** Every remaining
route runs through the fibre's own colour and through `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-22-hair-frame-design.md`
§5's per-channel `T_f = d_f · Π ā_f(θ_d)`, where chromaticity sharpens geometrically with depth
while level falls. `ā_f` was already derived and bounded at ≤ 0.131 by `tools/critic/hair-af.mjs`.
That is the next lever, and it is a MATERIAL change rather than a rig one.
