- Compare two explicit next directions: **A**, an authored coupled garment correction that preserves the body and pose, or **B**, separately qualified body/pose/skin work where a concrete body constraint requires it. A limits product impact but may lack enough freedom; B provides more freedom and carries much broader appearance and motion obligations. Neither is selected by the current evidence alone.

Include source rest, the four retained runtime poses, front/rear/obliques and a predeclared moving sequence. Keep the failed candidate visible in the evidence so that progress and regressions remain legible.
