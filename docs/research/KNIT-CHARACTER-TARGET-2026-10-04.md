**Retained artist-discussion reference, October4.** One new synthetic concept gives the future wardrobe a relaxed long-sleeve knit and wider trouser silhouette, alongside clear face framing. Root and the independent critic actually inspected the unchanged original and retain it for that purpose. It does not implement a new avatar, wearable outfit or representative starter build.

Relaxed shoulders/full sleeves, an untucked ribbed hem, wider woven-looking trouser legs and distinct boot uppers/soles articulate a different shape from the current close tee/narrow-denim example. ## What the target clarifies

Use the picture to discuss shoulder room, sleeve volume, an intentional hem, trouser-to-boot balance, material separation and unobstructed eyes/brows/jaw. Preserve the calm pale-blue/charcoal/brown composition as a candidate direction. The clear part and crown-to-ends flow describe a new whole-character styling possibility; they do not replace Robert's original bob references or accepted assets.

The actual [COS clothing reference](https://www.cos.com/en-us/men/menswear/knitwear/jumpers/merino/product/boiled-merino-wool-crew-neck-jumper-blue-1338797005) informed matte wool, ribbed trims, relaxed shoulders and wide trouser folds. Two original browser photo observations were independently inspected. The untucked hem, charcoal controlled trouser break, brown boots and original face/bob are artist choices. See [reference provenance](../evidence/appearance-2026-10-04/knit-character-concept/REFERENCES.md); the failed Uniqlo fetch supplied no visual evidence.
