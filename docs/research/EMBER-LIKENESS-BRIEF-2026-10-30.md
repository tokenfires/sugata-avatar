## Review without moving the goalposts

Review likeness, visual finish and expression meaning as separate questions. All matter; passing one does not cancel a failure in another.

- Compare actual Sugata portraits against both close references, including frontal and three-quarter views at useful viewing size. A profile helps establish depth once a model exists. Judge the head and face without allowing the background, clothing or hair colour to carry the identity claim.
- The reviewer must name concrete matches and mismatches in the regions above and point to the actual reference and candidate view. The root independently sees those same images and checks the critic's handoff. Missing or hidden evidence stays unassessed, not passed.
- Allow differences caused by real-time rendering: fewer visible individual hairs, simplified pore detail, different depth of field or background, and modest material/lighting variation. Those allowances do not excuse a different face, wrong eye spacing/shape, absent main freckles, incorrect hair silhouette, floating eyes, broken eyelids or obvious skin/scalp artifacts.
- Render a warm softly lit presentation and an ordinary neutral-light diagnostic of the same model when a new native stage is authorized. Neutral geometry should be derived from the portrait and rig. Do not ask Ember for another generated deadpan studio face merely to satisfy a capture recipe. The sheet's explanation of generation behavior is author-reported context, not an independently established model diagnosis.
- Keep a simple per-area record: matches, needs work, or not assessable, with image witnesses. Do not invent a combined percentage that can hide a major identity failure. Ask Robert and Ember whether the whole result reads as her; human judgment remains part of acceptance.
- For future candidates, recommend bounded evidence-led revisions rather than lowering the likeness target. Preserve failed versions and explain each proposed correction. This reference intake neither reopens prior closed recipes nor changes their frozen evidence.
