## Fresh coverage operator

casual hip-mask additions. Use current exact foundation bytes and original mask calibration;
do not regenerate flags to make a candidate pass. Count the actual new workload explicitly.

The operator is defined before any new candidate is solved:

1. Work in one shared posed/world coordinate space. Query each selected foundation triangle's
   centroid against all full-body triangles by minimum Euclidean point-to-triangle distance.
   Resolve exact ties by lowest body triangle ID; use that triangle's winding-derived geometric
   normal. Refuse degenerate or unclassifiable inputs.
2. Choose the world axis least aligned with the normal (X/Y/Z tie order). Set
   `u = normalize(axis × normal)`, `v = normal × u`. Project into this orthonormal chart.
3. Expand the foundation triangle footprint by the Minkowski sum with the chart-aligned
   ±1 mm square, rather than just expanding its bounding rectangle.
4. Clip outer triangles against the inclusive −4 mm / +30 mm centroid-normal depth slab
   before projection. Include only outward-facing triangles with geometric-normal dot chart
   normal strictly positive. Compute the union of their projected cover.
5. Uncovered expanded-footprint area must be at most **1e-14 m²**. Document numerical precision
   and degeneracy handling; do not silently swallow small holes with a larger geometric epsilon.

The nearest query/tie rule and tangent-axis convention were not fully specified in the retained
historical contract. They are explicit NEW protocol choices; no historical replay equivalence
is asserted. Meaningful analytic tests must cover overlap, seams, small holes, reversed faces,
depth-slab boundaries and deliberately removed cover. Independently critique the operator before
using it as acceptance evidence. Evaluate authored geometry plus the fresh posed workload for
