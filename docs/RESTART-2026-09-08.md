## Lay of the land

The project aims at a high-quality, expressive body for an AI, including clothing and identity
choice. The original ambition remains in `docs/BRIEF.md`; this restart does not lower it.

| Area | Current implementation and boundary |
|---|---|
| Runtime | `packages/core/src/Avatar.js` composes the figure, materials, scene and motion through a public API. |
| Rendering | Three.js WebGPU, structural quality tiers, skin/eye/hair materials, temporal resolve and a WebGL downgrade. No demonstrated runtime frame-budget governor. |
| Embodiment | Central motion composition includes breath, blink, gaze, idle, PAD affect/posture, gesture, IK and supplied viseme timelines. |
| Conversation | Lexicon/prosody appraisal and an optional LM Studio-compatible client exist. The local-model proxy is a dev-server facility. |
| Voice | Timeline consumption exists; real TTS and microphone capture remain unbuilt. `say(text)` alone does not produce phoneme timing. |
| Identity/clothing | Dedicated systems and testbed pages exist; detailed identity and wardrobe are not wired into `Avatar`. |
| Assets | Five figure bakes and seven hair styles are present. `bob01` covers all five bakes; the other styles have a midpoint bake. Availability does not certify visual quality. |
| Tooling | Extensive selftests, browser captures, quantitative critics and experimental ledgers. Some claims depend on unavailable references; declared red gates remain. |
