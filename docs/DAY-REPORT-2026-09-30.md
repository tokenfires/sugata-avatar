## What you can use

**Hair colour now has a separate tint and amount.** Keep a brown, blonde or other base shade underneath a coloured tint, adjust its strength, and retain both choices in a saved look. This builds on last night's seven starting shades and lightness control.

**Clothing colours can be mixed individually.** Top, Bottom and Shoes can each use a custom colour while retaining their fabric detail. Hair and clothing choices travel together through settings files and browser-local named looks. The two current outfits remain the supported wardrobe examples.

**Neutral Studio makes colour inspection easier.** Its light sources are achromatic, reducing the strong blue highlights on shoes and skin. It is an optional saved look setting; the authored floor still has its own colour. It is not calibrated colour reproduction.

**The review links now open their editors directly.** Hair and clothing links land on the matching open section with keyboard focus ready to enter the controls. Desktop/mobile entry and preservation of applied settings and an unsaved draft are independently reviewed.

These features are integrated locally and independently reviewed from actual desktop/mobile controls and rendered examples. Exact screenshot reproduction remains unresolved, and immediate high-quality lighting transitions are not fully qualified.
