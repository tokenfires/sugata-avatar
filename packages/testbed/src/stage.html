G-buffer readback probe

Each channel is read back with readRenderTargetPixelsAsync and checked against a prediction that a still frame, or an unwritten attachment, would fail.

Velocity vs motion

A velocity buffer that is uniformly zero on a moving object is the classic silent failure, and a uniformly-zero one on a static frame is what proves the measurement means anything. Both directions are run.

GPU cost

Timestamp-query durations at 1920x1080, dpr 1, on a separate offscreen renderer — the same conditions as the Phase 0 spikes in tools/spikes, so the numbers sit beside the 3.604 ms four-RectAreaLight figure without conversion.
