Identity sculpting browsercheck — punch list 10.1, 10.2

203 slider categories across 21 regions, read out of MPFB's own target.json. A target is a pure additive per-vertex offset with no solver, so an identity is applied once, on the CPU, into the position buffer — it is not a morph target and it costs nothing per frame. The panel below the viewport measures that claim rather than asserting it.

Presets

Sliders

66 of the 203 categories are has_left_and_right and draw two widgets, so the exposed set is 200 sliders and 266 widgets. Symmetry is the default; the left/right split is 10.12's item and is not on this page.

Measured, live

Per-frame cost

Identity writes into POSITION and touches no morph target, so the figure's GPU morph budget is the same number before and after. Press the button to sample frame time on the current identity; compare it against the neutral figure's.

What this page does NOT yet do

The eyes, cornea, teeth, tongue, brows and lashes are separate meshes fitted to the body by MPFB's barycentric rule, and the skeleton is placed from the body's own vertices. Neither is refitted here — that is punch list 10.7 and 10.9. Move a head or body slider far enough and the eyeballs stay where they were. The numbers below say how far the body moved so the size of that gap is visible rather than implied.
