Spike 0.10 — RectAreaLight cost curve on a 100k-triangle physical mesh

RectAreaLight is the single biggest driver of the portrait look and it has no cheap path: it casts no shadows, and three.js's clustered lighting filters to isPointLight && !castShadow, so area lights are never clustered. Every light is an LTC evaluation in every lit fragment. That makes the cost linear in lights × covered pixels and unavoidable. This page finds where the classic key/fill/rim/kicker rig starts to hurt.
