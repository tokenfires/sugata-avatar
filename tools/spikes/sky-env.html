Spike 11.2 — does scene.environment reach a surface under WebGPURenderer?

docs/CHECKPOINT.md §7 measures image-based light at 0.00% of a forehead pixel, because scene.environment and environmentNode are both null. Every skin, eye and hair number in this repository was measured with no IBL at all. Punch-list 11.2 proposes to change that with SkyMesh → cube → PMREMGenerator.fromScene() → scene.environment, which is unfamiliar in this stack and therefore gets a spike before it gets a line inside packages/.

🚩 A sky drawn behind the figure proves nothing. The question this page exists to answer is whether the environment reaches a surface, and the only proof of that is turning the environment off and watching the surface go black while the sky stays bright. §A is that proof, and it is deliberately reported next to the whole-frame mean that cannot see it.

Environment

§A — Does the environment reach a surface? RED PROOF

Every row is one arm rendered into a 640×640 linear half-float target with NoToneMapping, so the numbers are scene-referred radiance and not a tone curve's opinion of it. disc mean is the mean luma inside the analytically projected disc of one sphere, at 0.75 of its screen radius; frame mean is the mean over the whole 640×640 target. Read the two columns against each other in the sky-bg, env OFF row.

§B — What does the bake cost?

submit is wall time inside fromScene() — CPU work plus command encoding. to GPU idle additionally awaits device.queue.onSubmittedWorkDone(), so it is the number a scene change actually stalls for. first is the very first bake at that size and carries the blur/GGX shader compile; every later bake is the steady-state figure.

§C — What does it cost per frame?

GPU timestamps, median over repeated interleaved passes, at the page's canvas size. The figure is HIDDEN for this sweep so the sky covers close to the whole frame — a subject covering the frame would understate the background's cost, and the worst case is what a budget needs.

§D0 — Does the live environment survive a re-bake? GOTCHA HUNT

suggest() changes time of day at runtime, so re-baking into a live scene.environment is not an edge case — it is the feature. Two columns per step: disc mean is whether the sphere is lit, atlas mean is the PMREM render target read straight back, so an empty environment map and a broken binding can be told apart. They have opposite fixes, and the first three runs of this page chased the wrong one of them.

§E — The sun model, and whether the key agrees with the sky

Derived in sky-sun.js from SkyMesh's own constants rather than chosen by eye. The validation arms ask the only question that matters: does an analytic key light standing in for the hidden solar disc put the same light on the subject that the disc itself would have?

§F — Do background and environment want the same texture?

A PMREM atlas can be assigned straight to scene.background — three routes it through pmremTexture() (NodeManager.updateBackground, r185) — so the tempting simplification is one texture for both jobs. This section puts the sun IN FRAME and measures the frame's peak luma both ways. A prefiltered cube is a low-pass filter; the question is how much of the sky it costs, and the answer depends entirely on whether the sky has anything small in it.
