**One sentence:** the hair is muddy because the pedestal — 65.4% of the groom's energy — is

## 1. The four arms, one table

Fibre-axis saturation of the pedestal term, mass-weighted over 7,913 visible hair samples (signed
statistic: positive = toward the fibre's warm; a violet gain scores negative — REQ-063 §6's lesson,
gated in the selftest):

| arm | what its `n` is | gain | luma cost |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| **full signal** | true per-(pixel, light) ray-cast crossings | **+109.2%** | 0.574× |
| **pixel-shuffled** | same, pixel placement destroyed, light structure kept | +112.2% (**103%** of full) | — |
| **per-light constants** | each light's MEDIAN n only: `[key 0, fill 1, rim 26, kicker 11]` | **+76.8%** (70% of full) | 0.682× |
| **sheet** | the existing baked sheet (light-blind, random) | **+1.5%** | 0.633× |

G-BOUNDARY exact (0.0e+0). Wrap-sensitivity: same sign at wrap ≡ 1 (+181%), so the fake-normal
approximation is not deciding anything.

## 2. 🎯 What the decomposition proves

- **Pixel placement is worth nothing.** Destroying it keeps 103% of the gain.
- **Light structure is worth everything.** The sheet — which has none — buys 1.5%.
- **And the aggregate is almost enough:** four medians carry 70% of the perfect-signal ceiling.

The mechanism, in the numbers: with per-light depth, the rim's contribution to the pedestal passes
through `√C^(1+26)` — annihilated — while the key (n=0) and fill (n=1) survive warm. **The muddiness
is the blue rim and kicker leaking into the pedestal through a term that cannot tell a front light
from a back one.** The pedestal is where 65.4% of the energy lives, which is why every *lobe*-level
fix failed: TT (closed geometrically), TRT-glint (REQ-064, the fake carries 88.8%), the rim's own
knobs (REQ-063/078). The polluted term was the fake itself.

## 3. The registered outcome, honestly

**G-DECOY failed as operationally registered** (shuffled ≫ 20% of ordered), and the registration §6
pre-committed the consequence: *the decoy is the finding.* Two corrections to my own registration's
prose, recorded rather than smoothed over:

1. The registration's parenthetical called the shuffle "the shipped sheet's own failure mode." It is
   not — the shuffle preserves per-light structure, the sheet has none. The **sheet arm** is the true
   test of that null, and it **confirms R27**: a light-blind random input buys nothing. What R27
   could not see is that *light*-structured depth was the missing property, not *pixel*-structured
   depth — its probe never had a per-light input to try.
2. The registered interpretation "decoy failure ⇒ R27's diagnosis is wrong" was too coarse for the
   same reason. R27's diagnosis stands for what it tested; the decomposition above is the sharper
   statement.

**The per-pixel signal-plumbing round is NOT licensed — because it is not needed.** The registered
floor asked whether a perfect signal clears 9.5%; it clears 109%, but four constants clear 77%, so
building the expensive signal to collect the last 30 points would be spending a `render/**` round on
a second-order term.

## 4. ⚠️ What this is not, yet

- **Term arithmetic, not a plate.** The statistic is of the pedestal term itself. On the final pixel
  it dilutes by the pedestal's ~65% share and by the grade; and the luma cost (0.68× on the term ≈
  −21% on the groom) means the constants arm **must be level-matched before it is judged**, or the
  A/B is "darker and warmer" against "brighter and muddy" — two variables, the recorded eight-round
  mistake.
- **The constants are portrait-rig constants.** `[0, 1, 26, 11]` are medians of *this* groom under
  *this* preset. A different preset or groom re-derives them from its own cast — they are measured
  per rig, not authored.
- The kicker's n=11 annihilates it from the pedestal almost as thoroughly as the rim; whether its
  fill role on the dark side survives that is a plate question, not an arithmetic one.

## 5. The successor round, named for whoever runs it

**R36: the constants arm on plates.** A defect arm (`?hairdefect=` — graph-build-gated like every
other, shipped path byte-identical) that multiplies each light's pedestal contribution by
`√C^(n_light)` with the four measured constants; **level-matched** to the shipped plate by the
registered luma-match procedure; judged blind with a decoy axis per `JUDGE-BRIEF.md`. The
registration should carry a signed colour statistic (this file's), the level-match tolerance, and
the A/B/decoy protocol. Its risk is one free variable (the level-match scalar), and the protection
is that R27 already built the level-matching discipline this project uses.

The ceiling above is what it is entitled to claim; the plate round decides what it actually looks
like.
